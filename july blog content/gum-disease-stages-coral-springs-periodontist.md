## **The Four Stages of Gum Disease**

Gum disease doesn't happen overnight. It progresses gradually, and each stage requires a different level of care.

**1\. Gingivitis (Early Stage)** This is the mildest and most reversible form of gum disease. Symptoms include red, swollen gums that bleed easily during brushing or flossing. At this stage, a professional cleaning and improved home care can often reverse the damage completely.

**2\. Early Periodontitis** When gingivitis isn't addressed, plaque and tartar build up below the gumline, causing the gums to start pulling away from the teeth and forming small pockets. Bone loss can begin here, though it's usually minor.

**3\. Moderate Periodontitis** Pockets deepen, bone loss becomes more noticeable, and teeth may start to feel loose or shift slightly. Patients often notice persistent bad breath and gum tenderness.

**4\. Advanced Periodontitis** This is the most serious stage, with significant bone and tissue loss. Teeth may become very loose or require extraction if left untreated.
