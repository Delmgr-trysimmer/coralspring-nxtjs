# **What Does a Periodontist Do? A Coral Springs Patient's Guide**

If your dentist has ever mentioned "periodontal" anything — pockets, bone loss, gum recession — you may have left the appointment with more questions than answers. What exactly does a [**periodontist in Coral Springs**](https://www.coralspringsmiles.com/periodontist) do, and how is that different from your regular dentist? At Coral Springs Smiles, [Dr. Payal Anand, DDS](https://www.coralspringsmiles.com/our-doctor), fields this question often, and the short answer is: a periodontist focuses specifically on the gums and the bone that anchors your teeth. Here's what that actually means for you as a patient.

## **A Periodontist Specializes in Gum and Bone Health**

General dentists handle a wide range of care — cleanings, fillings, crowns — but periodontal disease requires a more focused skill set. Periodontists complete additional training beyond dental school specifically in diagnosing and treating conditions that affect the gums, the ligaments holding teeth in place, and the jawbone underneath. Dr. Payal Anand completed a periodontics fellowship at LSU after graduating from LSU School of Dentistry, giving her advanced training most general dentists don't have. That background is exactly why patients throughout Coral Springs are referred to her, or seek her out directly, when gum concerns come up.

## **Diagnosing Gum Disease Before It Becomes a Bigger Problem**

One of the core things a periodontist does is measure and monitor "pocket depths" — the space between your gums and teeth. Deeper pockets usually mean more advanced gum disease. At [Coral Springs Smiles](https://www.coralspringsmiles.com/), a periodontal evaluation with Dr. Payal Anand includes a thorough look at your gum tissue, bone levels, and any areas of inflammation or recession. Catching these changes early is often the difference between a simple cleaning and a more involved treatment plan down the road.

## **Treating Gum Disease with Scaling and Root Planing**

When plaque and tartar build up below the gumline, a routine cleaning often isn't enough to remove it. This is where **scaling and root planing** comes in — a deep cleaning procedure that clears out bacteria and smooths the tooth root so gums can reattach and heal. It's one of the most common treatments Dr. Payal Anand performs at Coral Springs Smiles, and for many patients, it's enough to stop gum disease from progressing further.

## **Advanced Options: Laser Dentistry and LANAP**

For patients with more advanced periodontal disease, Coral Springs Smiles offers **laser dentistry**, including LANAP-based treatment. Laser therapy allows Dr. Payal Anand to target infected tissue with precision, often with less discomfort and a faster recovery than traditional gum surgery. It's a good example of how periodontal treatment has evolved — patients no longer need to assume gum disease means a long, uncomfortable recovery.

A periodontist's role doesn't stop at natural teeth.
